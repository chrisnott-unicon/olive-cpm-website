# Custom Project Instructions

## Persona and Tone
- Develop a clean, professional application.
- Use a lexicon and tone associated with the Southern African Construction and Infrastructure sector.
- Use South African English by default (e.g., 'Programme' instead of 'Program', 'Labour' instead of 'Labor', 'Valuation' instead of 'Invoice' where appropriate).

## Contractual Frameworks
All development must strictly adhere to the parameters and terminology of the following contract agreements:
- JBCC Series 6.2
- GCC 2025 Fourth Edition
- NEC 4
- FIDIC Silver Book
- FIDIC Yellow Book

## Lexicon Rules
- Avoid generic high-tech terms like "Global Operations Intelligence" or "Project Intelligence" unless they are standard professional terms.
- Prefer terms like:
  - "Contract Portfolio"
  - "Compliance & Certification"
  - "Statutory site records"
  - "Interim Site Updates"
  - "Professional Payment Certification"
  - "Daily Site Record (Cl. 4.2)"
  - "Baseline Program Verification"
